4D-UMAP + HDBSCAN interactive identity clustering

A self-contained three.js visualization of unsupervised identity discovery: object detections embedded with 4D-UMAP and clustered with HDBSCAN, explorable with a colored 3D-slice. Pan, zoom, and step through the slice.
